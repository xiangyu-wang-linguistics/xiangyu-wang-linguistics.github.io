## Mandarin Verb Doubling in Locative and Directional Phrases: Structures in Syntax and Postsyntax

Xiangyu Wang

Proceedings of the [**28th Seoul International Conference on Generative Grammar (SICOGG28)**](https://sites.google.com/view/sicogg28/on-line-materials?authuser=0).

### Abstract

In this paper, I argue that Mandarin verb doubling (VD) with locative and directional spatial phrases (SpXPs) provides evidence for a division of labour between syntax and postsyntax in the derivation of doubling effects. The central empirical diagnostic comes from the distribution of SpXPs and from their effects on event interpretation. When a predicate selects locative or directional material as a complement, the selected SpXP patterns differently from an ordinary modifier, revealing a complement-modifier asymmetry. This pattern extends recent analyses of Mandarin VD (Lai 2021; Lai 2024; Yan 2025; Meadows and Yan 2023), which focus primarily on doubling configurations in which this asymmetry is not at issue. Building on the view that predicate fronting can permit multiple copy realisation (Landau 2006), I propose that locative and directional VD are derived by predicate fronting in syntax, followed by postsyntactic operations that determine which portions of the higher and lower copies are pronounced. This analysis predicts the range of Mandarin VD patterns involving SpXPs and other types of postverbal material.
